# Privacy

Velochrome collects no data about you or your photos, and makes no network connections.

- It works only with the local files you open: your raw photos and the `.xmp` sidecars beside
  them, which hold your ratings, labels and edits, and the JPEGs you export.
- On your PC it keeps a few settings (window layout, theme, recent folders, export options), a
  cache of thumbnails and previews, and a crash log, all in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Velochrome` (in the
  Microsoft Store version, in the app's private folder, removed when you uninstall it). Nothing in
  these is sent anywhere.
- It has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry and no advertising, and it does not check for
  updates itself. (If you install it from the Microsoft Store, Microsoft's Store delivers updates
  under its own privacy statement.)
- Camera profiles are read from the Adobe files already on your PC, if there are any.

Because nothing leaves your PC, there is nothing to delete or export from us. If this ever changes,
this file will say so, and the change will be in the project's history.
